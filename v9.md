    # Upgrade prompt (v9) — Local Shop OS: premium landing page redesign

Paste this into Antigravity as a follow-up brief, after v1–v8 are built. **This pass is 100% focused on the public landing page** (`/`) — do not touch the storefront, tenant dashboard, or super admin panel in this pass. The goal: a landing page that looks and feels like a premium, professionally designed SaaS product page — not a template, not "obviously AI-built." Take your time on this one and use real design judgment, not just a checklist.

---

## Design direction

- **Color palette**: yellow, white, and black. Dark backgrounds are allowed and encouraged where they make sections feel more premium (e.g. a dark hero or a dark pricing/CTA section for contrast), as long as the yellow brand color and white space are still used purposefully elsewhere — this should not become an all-dark theme, it's yellow/white/black used deliberately, section by section, for visual rhythm.
- **Feel**: premium, confident, fast, modern — think of the polish level of top-tier SaaS marketing sites, not a free template. Avoid generic centered-text-block-after-centered-text-block layouts; vary section composition (asymmetric layouts, overlapping elements, large confident typography, generous whitespace).
- **Typography**: pick a strong, modern typeface pairing (a bold display font for headlines, a clean readable font for body text) and use scale deliberately — huge, confident headline sizes in the hero, clear hierarchy throughout.
- It must not look AI-generated or generic — avoid default component patterns, avoid stock-looking icon grids with no personality, avoid the "3 identical cards in a row" cliché unless intentionally styled to feel distinctive.

## Logo

- Generate a proper logo for the platform as part of this pass, in yellow, specific to what this system actually is (a local-shop e-commerce/ordering platform) — not a generic abstract mark. Base it on the direction already discussed (a simplified cart/shop/storefront icon), refined to a genuinely polished, professional standard this time.
- Apply it in the landing page header/nav and favicon as part of this redesign.

## Structure and sections

1. **Splash/loading screen**: a brief branded loading transition on first load (logo animates in, then transitions smoothly into the page) — keep it fast (well under a second or two), it should feel premium, not like a delay.
2. **Sticky animated header/nav**: logo, nav links (Features, Pricing, About, Contact), a clear CTA button. Should visually react to scroll (e.g. background/blur/shadow appears once scrolled past the hero). Mobile: collapses to a clean animated menu (not an abrupt show/hide — a smooth open/close transition).
3. **Hero section**: this is the most important section — big confident headline, a clear supporting line explaining the value prop, primary CTA ("Start free trial") and secondary CTA. Include a real animated element here (not just static text) — e.g. animated text reveal, a subtle floating/parallax visual, or an animated product/dashboard preview mockup.
4. **How it works**: 3-step flow (add products → share your shop link → get orders on WhatsApp), presented with motion/interaction as items scroll into view, not static icons.
5. **Features section**: highlight the platform's real capabilities (product catalog, WhatsApp ordering, storefront, ads, plans) with strong visuals — consider a more editorial layout than a plain grid (e.g. alternating image/text rows, or a scroll-linked showcase).
6. **Pricing section**: pull live from the `plans` table (per v7) — present clearly, with the most attractive plan visually emphasized, and make sure this section still looks intentional even with dynamic data (handle varying numbers of plans and feature-list lengths gracefully).
7. **About section**: short section on what the platform is for and who it's for (small local shop owners) — this can be a good place to use a darker, more editorial section treatment.
8. **Testimonials/social proof**: keep as clearly-marked placeholder content until real shops exist (do not fabricate fake reviews as if real), but style the section to feel finished and ready.
9. **FAQ section**: expandable accordion-style Q&A, smooth open/close animation.
10. **Contact form section**: name, email/phone, message fields, clear submit action with a success-state confirmation (toast or inline message) — style it to match the rest of the page, not like a bare default form.
11. **Footer**: sitemap-style links, support contact number (from `platform_settings`, per v8), social links if applicable, legal placeholders (Terms/Privacy), and the platform branding.

## Motion and interaction

- Smooth scrolling across the whole page (buttery, not jumpy).
- Scroll-triggered reveal animations on every major section (continue and elevate what was started in v7) — fade/slide/scale-in as sections enter the viewport, staggered for groups of elements (e.g. feature cards animate in one after another, not all at once).
- Micro-interactions on buttons and interactive elements (hover states, press states, subtle scale/color transitions) — nothing should feel static or unresponsive to the cursor/touch.
- **Custom cursor** on desktop: a subtle custom cursor effect (e.g. a soft circular follower, or a cursor that changes/grows when hovering over clickable elements) — desktop only, must not affect touch devices, and must not interfere with usability (never hide the ability to see where you're clicking).
- Keep all animation subtle and purposeful — this should read as "premium and considered," never as clutter, distraction, or something that gets in the way of reading content or tapping a button.

## Performance

- Despite the animation richness, the page must still load fast — lazy-load below-the-fold images and heavy visual assets, keep animation libraries lightweight, avoid render-blocking resources.
- Test that animations run smoothly on a mid-range mobile device, not just desktop — degrade gracefully (simpler/fewer animations) on lower-powered devices if needed rather than causing jank.

## Mobile responsiveness

- This premium feel must carry through fully to mobile — not a stripped-down fallback version. Smooth scroll, section animations, the animated mobile menu, and the hero's animated element should all have a mobile-adapted version that still feels premium, not just "functional."
- Re-verify at mobile (~375px), tablet (~768px), and desktop (~1280px+) specifically for this rebuild — this page has had repeated mobile issues in past passes, treat it as the top priority to get right this time, not an afterthought after desktop is done.

## What NOT to change in this pass
- Do not touch the storefront, tenant dashboard, super admin panel, plan system, or ad system — this pass is landing page only.
- Do not remove the SEO work from v8 (title/meta/OG tags, sitemap, semantic headings) — preserve it while restyling the page around it.

A fully rebuilt, premium-feeling landing page — splash screen, animated sticky nav, animated hero, motion throughout every section, custom cursor on desktop, a proper generated logo in yellow, live pricing data, a working contact form, and full mobile responsiveness with the same premium quality as desktop — loading fast and feeling distinctly designed rather than templated or AI-generated.
